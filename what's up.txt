	furniture and homewares references
		forget about all the requirements and todos and plans. this is furniture hours.

		tsuitate : free-standing panel dividers or screens
			can incorporate a variety of materials, such as paper, shouji, straw, bamboo, cloth, reeds, panel or carved wood, ..
			often purely decorative, if not to deter or direct vagrants in the home, or simply as a gate if outside
			often placed at points where visitors should note to remove their shoes, such as at engawa verandah entries or genkan steps
			outdoors varieties will use weatherproof materials

		noren : fabric curtains
			found in sizes ranging from short runners, half-length panels, full-length curtains, or sail-sized spreads
			may be decorated with calligraphy, iconography, or logography
			coloured in a variety of styles, traditionally blue or red
			often placed in back-doors, as well as kitchen, bedroom, or shop entry doorways

		sudare : bamboo-fibre outdoor blinds
			used in summer to shield from the sun, likely unused in other seasons
			pleasant, light colour
			thin craft, may be spaced to allow some light to pass through
			with borders, becomes misu, an article found in shrines and temples, occasionally in tassles

		hi-mousen : red felt spreads
			used to cover tables and benches for gifts, offerings, presentations, ..
			may be seen under home dolls, important wares, treasures, butsudan, ..
			may also be found in more colloquial settings, such as mats or picnic blankets
			bold crimson-vermillion colour
			
		komo : simple mats
			made of a rush, similar in appearance to straw/burlap or bamboo fibre
			coloured in beige
			coarse edges, often bare rush ends
			spartan and basic, largely undecorated and outmoded by mushiro

		mushiro : quality mats
			often patterned, colour including beige and faded reds, greens, and orange tones
			clean, lined edges

		enza : simple cushions
			basic unfilled straw cushions of coarse material
			raw material, beige colour
			often limited to shrines and temples, places of reserved comfort
			shaped as a rounded coaster or placemat

		zabuton : quality cushions
			stuffed cotton cushions
			surface often patterned and lined
			found in all homes in place of chairs or raised seating
			
		endai : benches
			used as table surfaces as often as seating, and often seating kneeled
			popular in bamboo or cut wood
			described as having use as a portable verandah

		ton : stools
			acceptable raised seating, often as a simple, single shape
			traditionally found as featureless rope-wound cylinders, enduring to the modern day
